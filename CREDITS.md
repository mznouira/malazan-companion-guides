# Credits

## The guides

Every word of the chapter summaries is the work of **u/sleepinxonxbed**
(@SonAnomander), who wrote the companion guides for r/Malazan and shares them
as Google Slides decks. This repository is a reader for that work, not a
rewrite of it — the summaries, the scene breakdowns, and the red/orange/blue
highlighting convention are all his.

- Original decks: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1NbYzA6NPk3u_osoL3J6P9jFDj6PYMHrQ
- r/Malazan thread: https://www.reddit.com/r/Malazan/comments/1f4e4hy/malazan_guides_and_pdfs/
- Tip jar: linked from the first slides of each deck

**Published here with his permission.** If that ever changes, open an issue and
the book data comes down the same day.

## Artwork

The decks include character art, maps, and illustrations by many different
artists, credited on the slides where the author knew the source. Nothing here
is original artwork and no ownership is claimed. Artists who would like a piece
removed or credited differently: open an issue.

## The books

*The Malazan Book of the Fallen* is by **Steven Erikson**, published by Bantam
(UK) and Tor (US). These guides are a companion to the novels, not a substitute
— read the chapter first.
